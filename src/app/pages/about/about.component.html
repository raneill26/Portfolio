About Me

I'm Ryan, a recent computer science graduate from UNC Chapel Hill, now working as a founding associate consultant at Variable Sync. Most of my day is spent inside incentive compensation systems: turning a sales plan written in prose into crediting rules, quotas, and payout logic, most recently in CaptivateIQ.

That work sits on top of the same foundations I built writing MIPS assembly and JavaFX games in school. Understanding the constraints, modeling problems cleanly, and testing until it performs. Additionally, I started development on an iOS appcalled Drinko. Outside of work, I love baseball, roller coasters, and Marvel movies.
